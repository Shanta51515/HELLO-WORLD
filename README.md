# How to Send an Email Through Gmail

## 1. Accessing Gmail

To send an email through Gmail, you first need to access your Gmail account. Follow these steps:

1. **Open a Web Browser**  
   Launch a web browser such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

2. **Go to the Gmail Website**  
   Type `https://mail.google.com` into the address bar and press **Enter**.

3. **Enter Your Email Address**  
   On the Gmail sign-in page, type your full Gmail email address (e.g., `yourname@gmail.com`) and click **Next**.

4. **Enter Your Password**  
   Type your password and click **Next** again.

5. **Access Your Inbox**  
   Once signed in, you will be redirected to your inbox where you can read, compose, and send emails.

> 📝 **Note:** If you're using a mobile device, you can also access Gmail through the **Gmail app**, available on both Android and iOS platforms.
